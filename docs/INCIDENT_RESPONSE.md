# Incident and personal-data breach runbook

1. Record detection time, reporter, affected systems and evidence; preserve logs without exposing secrets.
2. Contain access, revoke affected sessions/credentials and isolate the tenant or feature.
3. Determine data categories, people, organizations, jurisdictions, duration and likely consequences.
4. Notify affected customer controllers without undue delay with facts, mitigation and contact point.
5. The controller assesses supervisory-authority notification within 72 hours and notification to individuals where high risk exists.
6. Restore from a verified clean backup, monitor recurrence and document every decision.
7. Complete root-cause analysis, corrective actions and the breach register. Review subprocessors and DPA duties.

Contacts, escalation owners, supervisory authority details and out-of-hours procedures must be filled before launch.
